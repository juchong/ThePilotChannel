Cameras (live video)

A view of live camera streams. Each tile plays a stream slot, cam1 to cam12; the camera URLs live in .env as HANGAR_CAM1 to HANGAR_CAM12 (see the README). Tiles fill rows of six columns; a two-column tile is 16:9 and a four-column tile 32:9.

NOTAMs

One view per location, showing the NOTAMs that affect it: those in effect now and those taking effect within the look-ahead, grouped by type with issue and effective times in UTC (Z). A location is the identifier of an airport, NAVAID or ARTCC (S50 or KS50, KSEA, ZSE). Live data comes from the FAA NOTAM Management Service; its client ID and secret go in .env (see the README).

type one or more identifiers, then Enter

Screen

Step the display through its views or black out the picture, from here or from an automation (for example Home Assistant): POST /api/display/next, /previous, /view, /blackout, and /restore; see the README for ready-made rest_commands. These controls call the same endpoints.
